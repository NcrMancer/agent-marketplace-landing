The Problem

Everyone's building agent marketplaces. Nobody can trust them.

⚠

Zero verified transactions

A 270-post study of Moltbook (1.5M+ agents) found zero observable agent-to-agent transactions. The demand exists informally — but marketplaces can't capture it.

🔓

Supply chain attacks

1,184 malicious skills found in one registry. A fake skill passed all security scanners and reached 26,000 agents. No cross-platform verification exists.

✓

Proven demand, unserved

Anthropic's experiment: 186 real deals, 46% willingness to pay. Enterprise intent: 93%. The gap isn't demand — it's trust infrastructure.
